About Our Company

Nearly three decades of excellence in commercial vehicle fabrication, metallurgical integrity, and heavy automotive manufacturing.

Who We Are

Engineering Heavy Commercial Strength Since 1998

Founded as a dedicated commercial vehicle body manufacturing unit, Truck Body Builder has grown into an industry-leading fabrication hub covering 120,000 square feet with a monthly delivery throughput of over 85 commercial bodies.

We work in direct coordination with fleet corporations, logistics operators, state transport undertakings, mining conglomerates, and authorized OEM dealers for Tata Motors, Ashok Leyland, BharatBenz, and Eicher. Every chassis that rolls out of our facility is engineered with FEA structural modeling, certified welding, and ARAI roadworthiness approval.

ARAI / CMVR Certified

Full compliance with AIS-093 truck body codes.

OEM Direct Approvals

Authorized chassis body fabricator status.

Our Mission

To fabricate the most structurally reliable, lightweight, and legally compliant commercial vehicle bodies that maximize payload profitability and guarantee operator safety on every highway.

Our Vision

To be the benchmark commercial vehicle fabrication company recognized globally for metallurgical innovation, automated manufacturing, and zero-defect delivery standards.

Quality Policy

Zero tolerance for structural compromise. We use prime-tested steel certified with mill test certificates (MTC), ultrasonic weld inspections, and multi-stage anti-corrosion chemical treatment.

Our Journey

28 Years of Manufacturing Milestones

From a modest 2-bay workshop to a heavy engineering fabrication powerhouse.

1 1998

Foundation of Engineering Workshop

Started operations with standard wooden-composite and mild steel open cargo bodies on 16-ton chassis platforms.

2 2006

Expansion to Hydraulic Tipper Manufacturing

Commissioned specialized heavy tipping jigs, partnered with European hydraulic cylinder manufacturers, and secured major mining fleet contracts.

3 2014

ISO 9001:2015 & ARAI Code Homologation

Upgraded production to comply fully with AIS-093 truck code regulations and secured OEM Tier-1 fabrication accreditation.

4 2020

CNC Robotic Line & Laser Cutting Facility

Installed 12kW fiber laser cutting and 800-ton CNC bending press brakes, achieving 0.1mm tolerance across all sheet metal body components.

5 Present Day

Cross-Border Heavy Commercial Expansion

Surpassed 14,500 fabricated commercial bodies with turnkey deliveries for defense, e-commerce reefer fleets, and interstate multi-axle trailers.
