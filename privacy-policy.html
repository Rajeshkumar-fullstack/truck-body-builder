Data Protection & Confidentiality

Privacy Policy

Our commitment to protecting corporate fleet data, custom vehicle fabrication blueprints, chassis specifications, and quotation inquiries.

Effective Date: November 01, 2026 | Version 4.2

1. Introduction & Corporate Commitment

At Truck Body Builder & Commercial Vehicle Fabrication Works, we recognize the vital importance of commercial data privacy and technical confidentiality. Whether you are an owner-driver, a regional transport operator, an interstate logistics fleet company, or a commercial vehicle OEM distributor, we treat your business specifications, chassis details, and commercial transactions with rigorous industrial-grade security.

This Privacy Policy outlines how our web portal, digital quotation engines, engineering desk, and workshop management systems collect, utilize, store, and safeguard your corporate and operational information.

2. Information We Collect

When you interact with our website, request custom body quotations, purchase accessories in our shop, or consult with our metallurgists, we may collect the following categories of information:

  • Commercial Vehicle & Chassis Information: Vehicle make and model (e.g., Tata Signa, Ashok Leyland AVTR, BharatBenz, Eicher), chassis wheelbases, GVW ratings, axle configurations, and intended haulage applications.
  • Technical Drawings & Design Specifications: CAD blueprints, dimensional specifications, custom cargo drawings, and special application parameters submitted for quotation or homologation audits.
  • Corporate & Contact Details: Company/Fleet legal name, contact person name, corporate email address, verified 10-digit telephone/mobile number, GSTIN registration, and facility or dispatch address.
  • Transaction & Order Data: Accessories and hardware ordered through our online shop, quotation inquiry numbers, billing records, and delivery consignment notes.
  • Digital Telemetry & Session Data: Browser preferences saved locally on your device (including Dark/Light mode theme state, Left-to-Right or Right-to-Left orientation settings, and pending shopping cart item records).

3. How We Use Your Information

The collected information is utilized strictly to provide precision commercial bodybuilding services:

  • Generating Engineering Quotations: Calculating exact material requirements (e.g., Hardox 450 plates, Domex 700 ribs, structural steel beams) and calculating FEA stress simulations.
  • ARAI & CMVR Homologation: Preparing type-approval documentation conforming to AIS-093 (Revision 1) code of practice for RTO vehicle registration passing.
  • Production & Fabrication Scheduling: Managing workshop floor bays, CNC laser cutting, press-brake forming, and automated submerged arc welding pipelines.
  • Order Fulfillment & Spares Dispatch: Processing accessory purchases, arranging freight logistics, and tracking delivery timelines.
  • Warranty & Lifecycle Support: Maintaining vehicle structural records to honor our 5-year structural fabrication warranty and provide spare parts support.
Our Strict Zero-Data-Brokering Guarantee

We strictly guarantee that your technical drawings, fleet payload calculations, and company contact details are never sold, rented, leased, or traded to third-party telemarketers or external advertising networks. Your commercial engineering designs remain your exclusive intellectual property.

4. Confidentiality of Technical Blueprints & IP Protection

Commercial truck bodies often involve proprietary structural customizations—such as unique dropside locking mechanisms, specialized reefer partition geometries, or proprietary chassis extensions. Our engineering teams operate under strict non-disclosure practices:

  • All client CAD files, stress analysis reports, and bespoke chassis extension plans are stored on encrypted internal repositories accessible solely by authorized project engineers.
  • Drawings submitted for custom fabrication will never be shared with competing logistics operators or published online without express written consent.

5. Cookies & Local Browser Storage

Our website uses cookies and HTML5 Local Storage strictly to ensure smooth, functional browsing experiences:

  • truck_body_theme: Remembers your preferred display mode (Dark Theme vs. Light Theme).
  • truck_body_dir: Remembers your language direction layout (LTR vs. RTL).
  • truck_cart_items: Preserves your selected accessories, hardware, and quantities while you browse between pages.

You may clear your browser cookies and local storage at any time via your browser settings. Doing so will reset your theme, language, and cart selections to default values without affecting site accessibility.

6. Authorized Third-Party Coordination

To complete fabrication, testing, and delivery, certain data must be coordinated with certified industrial partners:

  • Chassis OEM Dealerships: Sharing wheelbase and mounting bracket clearances with authorized Tata Motors, Ashok Leyland, BharatBenz, or Volvo/Eicher service teams for chassis warranty continuity.
  • Accredited Testing Agencies: Sharing material test certificates with ARAI, CIRT, or ICAT testing officers during homologation audits.
  • Logistics & Transport Carriers: Providing shipping consignee contact details to freight carriers transporting completed truck bodies or heavy parts.

7. Data Security Safeguards

We implement robust physical and digital safeguards to protect commercial vehicle assets:

  • End-to-end 256-bit SSL/TLS protocol encryption across all website forms and quotation requests.
  • Restricted physical access to our engineering CAD lab and workshop administration facility.
  • Periodic security audits of our website infrastructure and quotation databases to prevent unauthorized intrusion.

8. Your Rights Regarding Your Information

Under applicable Indian data privacy regulations and international commercial standards, commercial clients hold the following rights:

  • Right to Review: Request a full copy of all technical quote records and customer details associated with your company account.
  • Right to Correction: Request updates or rectifications to contact persons, GST numbers, or delivery addresses.
  • Right to Erasure: Request the deletion of expired quotes and contact information once warranty and mandatory regulatory retention periods expire.
