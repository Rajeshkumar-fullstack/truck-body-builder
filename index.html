Streamlined Workflow

Our 4-Stage Manufacturing Process

From initial payload analysis to final RTO/ARAI certification, we adhere to strict ISO quality control protocols.

01
Design & Engineering

3D CAD design, weight distribution, axle load calculations, and OEM chassis compliance verification.

02
Precision Fabrication

CNC plasma cutting, press brake bending, subframe alignment, and certified MIG/SAW structural welding.

03
Surface & Coating

Automated shot blasting to SA 2.5 standard, zinc phosphate priming, and bake-cured polyurethane finish.

04
Testing & Dispatch

Static water leak tests, ultrasonic weld inspection, electrical harness check, and ARAI roadworthiness sign-off.
