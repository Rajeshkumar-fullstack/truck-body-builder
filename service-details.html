Commercial Vehicle Fabrication

Ground-Up Structural Engineering for All Commercial Chassis

We fabricate high-payload commercial truck bodies engineered to withstand dynamic torsional flexing, severe shock loading, and harsh highway environments across India.

Utilizing high-tensile hot-rolled steel plates (IS 2062 Grade E250 / E350 and Domex/Hardox alloys), our commercial bodies maximize legal cargo volume while minimizing tare curb weight. Every assembly adheres to AIS-093 Type-Approval requirements, guaranteeing hassle-free RTO passing.

The 4-Step Engineering Workflow

Step 1
Chassis CAD Integration

Direct 3D CAD modeling aligned with factory OEM chassis drawings (Tata, Ashok Leyland, BharatBenz, Eicher) to guarantee zero interference with fuel tanks and AdBlue SCR units.

Step 2
Automated Submerged Arc Welding

Longitudinal subframe channels are welded with automated submerged arc gantries (SAW) achieving full-depth root penetration without warping or heat-affected brittleness.

Step 3
Shot Blasting & Epoxy Coating

Total abrasive shot-blasting to Swedish Standard SA 2.5 removes all mill scale, followed by a two-pack zinc phosphate epoxy primer and 100-micron polyurethane topcoat.

Step 4
Chassis Mating & Homologation

Subframe is secured to chassis using high-tensile 10.9-grade U-bolts and wooden/polyurethane sleeper dampers. Electrical harness and reflective tape applied to CMVR standards.
