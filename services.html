Strict Testing Protocols

Industrial Quality Benchmarks

Every fabricated structure passes four mandatory structural checkpoints before dispatch.

100% Water Ingress Test

High-pressure overhead shower rig tests every closed container and reefer seal.

Ultrasonic Weld Inspection

Non-destructive testing (NDT) detects internal weld flaws and incomplete penetration.

Calibrated Axle Weighing

Certified weighbridge validates tare weight and compliance with legal unladen weight.

Full compliance documentation for seamless RTO registration in all Indian states.
